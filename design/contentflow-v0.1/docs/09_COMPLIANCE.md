# 09 — Rights, evidence, editorial and platform compliance

## 1. Fail-closed policy model

Policy evaluation returns `ALLOW`, `REVIEW`, or `DENY`, plus rule ID/revision, reason, evidence IDs, required actor, expiry and remediation. Unknown rights, insufficient evidence or expired account rules must not produce an approved publication. A hard denial cannot be overridden by a generic human approval; revise content or provide new valid evidence. Rule snapshots immutable, effective date + checked date + next review + source URL; account facts verified independently. Publish re-evaluates current rules and asset/source dependencies. Automated checks are triage, not legal advice or a guarantee of monetization. Separate private draft, release-ready export, platform acceptance, ad suitability, eligibility and payout.

## 2. Rights ledger

Record for each input/output/derivative: rights holder/creator if known, asset/source ID and revision, license identifier and version, evidence reference (not necessarily full copyrighted text), acquisition time, allowed use (read, extract, quote, transform, commercial, distribute, AI input/training), geographic/time/platform/audience restrictions, attribution text, redistribution/derivative clauses, voice/likeness consent, expiration/revocation and reviewer. `UNKNOWN`, `RESTRICTED`, `ALLOWED`, `EXPIRED`, `REVOKED` states. Derived rights never expand automatically; the intersection of inherited restrictions governs. Separate software library license, model weights license, output terms and rights of depicted person/music/font/stock. Public access, API access and robots approval are not republication rights. Do not upload confidential sources to providers without owner and provider boundary approval. Citation is necessary where appropriate but does not cure copyright infringement. Quoting is limited by applicable law/license; do not implement a universal percentage safe harbor.

## 3. Release gates

| Gate | Machine checks | Human check | Block conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 acquisition | source scope, retention, MIME, provenance | unusual license/private data | prohibited collection, unknown boundary |
| G2 evidence | claim references, coverage, freshness, formulas | accuracy, disputes, material limitations | unsupported material claim |
| G3 originality | lineage, duplicate similarity, original-contribution field | meaningful own analysis, channel-level variety | read-only rewrite, near-duplicate spam |
| G4 asset | digest, rights scope, attribution, expiry | depiction, voice consent, context | unauthorized/unverified license |
| G5 technical | codec, dimensions, timing, decode, audio, captions | narrative quality, Korean glyphs, pronunciation | failed QC or stale dependency |
| G6 disclosure | metadata, sponsorship/affiliate/AI flag tri-state | actual relationship and synthetic material | required disclosure missing |
| G7 release | approval digest, target/account, mode, current policy/budget | explicit consent to side effect | expired rule/approval, unknown account/charge |
| G8 post-release | retention refresh, rights changes, corrections | correction/retraction choice | freeze reuse while unresolved |

An approval is a signed/recorded action by an authenticated owner, bound to exact artifact and relevant dependencies; it cannot be generated by an LLM or copied between revisions. Preview may contain draft-only materials only if rights allow that private use. Export-only is not permission to distribute. Release-ready packages include rights manifest and disclosure notes. All synthetic fixtures marked `TEST_ONLY` and never publishable.

## 4. Platform and jurisdiction checklist

- YouTube monetization requires original value; repeated templates or copied narration can be rejected independently of copyright permission (R03). YPP thresholds and Shorts maintenance are separate dated rules (R01, R02), not hardcoded assumptions. Track channel-level review and current account status. Future activity thresholds and updated contract acceptance require user action in Studio. Never accept terms autonomously.
- YouTube realistic AI/altered material may need disclosure (R04); current paid promotion and audience fields must be explicit (R05, R06). Disclose material relationships and obtain human approval. Distinguish public views from qualified/engaged metrics; don't derive payout from public co-views. Authorized statistics can be subject to 30-day reauthorization and deletion checks; other API data generally needs refresh or deletion, with category-specific exceptions (R08). Do not download third-party YouTube audiovisual content via API or aggregate across owners where prohibited. Use independent owned or authorized inputs instead.
- TikTok Direct Post own/team-only utility is treated as disabled; verify live official guidelines and app review before any implementation (R09). Manual posting first. Meta professional-account eligibility and app scopes must be checked (R15). No cookie automation bypass. Unsupported destinations always export-only.
- Korean operators: check current tax registration and income reporting, employment/side-business and IP rules, commercial advertising disclosures, privacy and consumer protection before sales. No automated tax advice/filing, no default fixed affiliate rate. For health/legal/finance/politics or material involving identifiable people/minors, require specialized review or keep private. No autonomous publication in high-risk categories. Counsel/tax review if commercial scale or uncertain obligations.

## 5. Data retention and correction

Expiry job evaluates per-source and per-asset scope; freeze dependent new release before expiry. Corrections create new source/claim/content revisions; audit impact on pending jobs, exports and published variants; show actionable review queue, not automatic replacement. Remote delete/unlist/correct is a separately approved action. Purge revoked data, caches and restore paths subject to applicable terms; preserve only legally permitted minimal evidence and settlement records. User must be able to inspect/export owned data and disconnect accounts. Evidence registry uses URLs plus locally permissible records, not wholesale copies of official terms.
